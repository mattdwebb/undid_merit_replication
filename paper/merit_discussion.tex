Lastly, we estimate average treatment effects using data from all groups and all necessary years per group. Panel C of Table \ref{tab:merit} reports results from UN-DID, CSDID, and DID-INT. For UN-DID, we treat each state's data as siloed and use differences without covariate adjustment. CSDID implements the procedure of \citet{callaway2021difference} and includes the indicators for male, black, and Asian individuals. DID-INT includes the same covariates and allows their coefficients to vary across states. For each method, we report a simple aggregation of cohort-time treatment effects and a group aggregation. The UN-DID and DID-INT calculations use sample-size weighting at both the difference and aggregation stages. Thus, these comparisons use different covariate specifications.

The reported standard errors use different jackknife procedures. For CSDID, we use the state-cluster jackknife implemented in \texttt{csdidjack}, following \citet{karim2026improvedinferencecsdidusing}. For UN-DID and DID-INT, we report a final-stage delete-one-subgroup jackknife: each deletion removes one of the 33 cohort-time estimates under simple aggregation, or one of the seven cohort estimates under group aggregation, and renormalizes the remaining weights. These subgroup jackknife standard errors do not implement state-level clustering, so differences in the reported standard errors reflect differences in inference procedures as well as estimation methods.

The simple aggregate ATTs are close: 0.0466 for UN-DID, 0.0464 for CSDID, and 0.0464 for DID-INT. Their reported standard errors are 0.0113, 0.0133, and 0.0102, respectively. Under group aggregation, the UN-DID and DID-INT ATTs both round to 0.0458, with standard errors of 0.0133 and 0.0084. The CSDID ATT is smaller, at 0.0339, with a state-cluster jackknife standard error of 0.0211; it is not statistically significant at the 10\% level using a two-sided normal approximation.

We also investigate the sensitivity of DID-INT to the remaining CCC assumptions in Appendix \ref{sec:merit_ccc}. The reported ATTs range from 0.0412 when allowing for time-varying coefficients to 0.0511 when allowing for two-way varying coefficients.
